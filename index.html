About Our Organization

Ujjayini Mahakal Tantra Sadhana Pith is deeply committed to preserving our rich spiritual heritage while actively participating in the upliftment of society through charitable, educational, and religious activities.

Dharmik Seva

Conducting regular pujas, tantra sadhana, and maintaining our sacred spaces for devotees to experience divine peace.

Samajik Karya

Organizing massive Annadaan (food distribution) and providing essential support to the marginalized communities.

Shikshan

Promoting educational initiatives and empowering the youth with traditional values and modern knowledge.
