About Our Sanstha

Serving Humanity Through Faith & Education

We are a registered charitable trust committed to the holistic development of society. Rooted in the ancient wisdom of Mahakal and Tantra Sadhana, we channel spiritual energy towards tangible social good.

Shikshan (Education)

Providing educational support and resources to underprivileged children.

Dharmik (Religious)

Preserving ancient Vedic traditions, rituals, and spiritual knowledge.

Samajik (Social Welfare)

Organizing health camps, Annadaan (food distribution), and community support initiatives for the marginalized.
